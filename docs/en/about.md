# About Traitly ⊹ ࣪ ˖

## Origins
Traitly started as an effort to develop an efficient and accessible phenotyping method for the internal structure of fruit, an area where computational tools are still limited. As the project grew, it expanded to include a module for external fruit appearance as well.

The tissue nomenclature used in Traitly (`total_pericarp`, `outer_pericarp`, `internal_pericarp`, `locules`) was introduced by Diaz-Garcia et al. (2019) in the context of American cranberry, and is adopted here with adaptations for a general fruit context.

> Diaz-Garcia, L., et al. (2019). Comprehensive analysis of the internal structure and firmness in American cranberry (*Vaccinium macrocarpon* Ait.) fruit. *PLOS ONE*, 14(9), e0222451. [https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0222451](__https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0222451__)

---
## Scope

Although Traitly was originally designed for cranberry, it was built to be modular and adaptable to different fruit morphologies and tissue types. It has been tested on tomato, orange, kiwi, dragon fruit, bell pepper, common bean, maize, and potato, among others.
